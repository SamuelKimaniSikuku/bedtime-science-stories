Voice Studio

Clone your voice once — then let it read every bedtime story automatically. Your API key and recordings go directly from this page to ElevenLabs; nothing is stored on the site.

← Back to the stories

1 · Connect your ElevenLabs account

Paste your API key (elevenlabs.io → Profile → API Keys). It is only kept in this browser.

2 · Your voice

Choose the voice that will read the stories:

Record a 1–2 minute sample in a quiet room, holding the phone/mic about 20 cm away. Speak warmly and naturally — like you're telling a story. You can read this (or anything you like, in any language):

Hello, my darling. Tonight I want to tell you a story about the stars. Long ago, people looked up at the night sky and wondered what those little lights could be. Some thought they were tiny holes in a great dark blanket. Others believed they were faraway fires. Slowly, patiently, curious people began to measure and count, to draw maps of the sky, and to ask why the moon changes shape and why the sun rises every morning. Every question they asked opened a little door, and behind every door there was another wonderful question. Close your eyes now, take a deep breath, and imagine you are floating gently among those stars, warm and safe, while the whole universe hums you a lullaby. Goodnight, my love. Sleep well, and dream big dreams.
