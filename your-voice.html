Record your voice — three short takes

Find a quiet room. Hold your phone about 20 cm from your mouth. Speak slowly, warmly — like you're tucking your little one in. You may read in any language you're comfortable with.

Three different passages give a much better voice than one: a calm one, a curious one, and a playful one. Each takes about a minute.



The email opens pre-filled — just attach the recording files you downloaded (check your Downloads folder) and press send. We'll reply with payment details and have your voice pack ready within a few days.

Questions parents ask

Is my voice safe? Yes. Your recording is used only to create your family's story narrations, is never shared, and is deleted on request.

Do I need any app or account? No — just this page, your voice, and an email.

What if I speak only one of the languages? No problem. Your recordings can be in any language — the stories can still be generated in all three, in your voice.

How do I pay? After you send your recording, we reply with M-Pesa or card payment options. You pay only when you're ready.
